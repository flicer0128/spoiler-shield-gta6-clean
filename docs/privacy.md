# Privacy Policy — Spoiler Shield: GTA 6

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Spoiler Shield does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data or browsing data.

- Page text is scanned locally in your browser only to decide what to blur. It never leaves your device.
- Your settings (on/off, blur/hide mode, custom keywords, paused sites) are saved with Chrome's built-in storage (chrome.storage.sync), which Google may sync across your own signed-in Chrome browsers.
- The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics or third-party services.

Questions: contact the developer via the email on the Chrome Web Store listing.
