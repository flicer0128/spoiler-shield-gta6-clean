## Store listing tab
**Name:** Spoiler Shield: GTA 6   (comes from manifest.json)

**Summary (from manifest):**
Blurs GTA 6 spoilers on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Reddit, Facebook, Threads, Twitch, Discord and more. Rated by spoiler level.

**Description:**
GTA 6 is finally out — and so are the spoilers. Spoiler Shield keeps your feeds safe until you've played it yourself.

WHAT IT DOES
• Covers any post, video, comment or headline that mentions GTA 6
• Rates every spoiler: Possible spoiler (yellow), Story spoiler (orange) or MAJOR/CRITICAL SPOILER (red)
• Choose your limit: block everything, or only story details, or only endings and twists
• Click to show any item, or switch to Hide mode to remove spoilers completely
• Warning screen before you open a page about GTA 6
• Add your own words at any level (mission names, a streamer, a friend who spoils things)
• Pause on any site with one click

WORKS ON
YouTube (including Shorts and live chat), TikTok, Instagram, X/Twitter, Reddit, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky, Tumblr, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Mastodon, Twitch, Kick, Discord, Telegram Web, WhatsApp Web, Steam Community, Google Search, and regular websites.

PRIVACY
Spoiler Shield never collects, stores or sends any of your data. All matching happens locally in your browser. Your settings are saved with Chrome's built-in sync storage.

## Privacy practices tab
**Single purpose:**
Hides or blurs web page content that mentions GTA 6, rated by spoiler level, so the user can avoid spoilers for the game.

**Permission justifications:**
- storage: Saves the user's settings (on/off, blur vs hide, custom keywords, paused sites).
- Host permission (content script on http/https pages): Spoilers can appear on any website, so the extension needs to read page text locally to find and blur GTA 6 mentions. No page content is stored or transmitted.
